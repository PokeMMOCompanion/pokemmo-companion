## PokeMMO Hub (pokemmohub.com)

Pokémon, move, ability, location, berry, breeding, egg-move and item data, the catch formula and PokeMMO catch
rates are a snapshot of PokeMMO Hub's tools (`app/src/main/assets/pokemmo/`, made with `tools/hub_snapshot.py`).
GTL prices are read live from PokeMMO Hub's price API (apis.fiereu.de).

## PokemonDB (pokemondb.net): sprites

Pokémon sprites are **not included**. When the user taps Download, the app fetches PokemonDB's Black/White sprites
onto the device, where they stay.
